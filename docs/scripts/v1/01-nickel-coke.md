# 1. The nickel Coke

**Hook (0-2s):** a nickel slaps down into a torn hole; clippings: "5¢ FOR 73 YEARS"

## VO
> For seventy-three years, a Coke cost exactly five cents. [3s]
> Through two world wars. Through the Great Depression. [3s]
> And Coca-Cola couldn't raise the price, even if it wanted to. [3s]
> Problem one: in 1899, two lawyers bought the rights to bottle Coke. Their contract locked in what they paid for the syrup. [6s]
> Problem two: vending machines. They took one coin. A nickel. [4s]
> The next coin up was a dime. That's doubling the price. [3s]
> So Coke's boss asked the President for a new coin. Seven and a half cents. [4s]
> The answer was no. [1.5s]
> The nickel Coke finally died in 1959. [3s]
