# 4. The molasses flood

**Hook (0-2s):** 1919 newspaper photo of wreckage, clippings "KILLED BY MOLASSES"

## VO
> In 1919, a wave of molasses killed twenty-one people in Boston. [4s]
> January fifteenth. A fifty-foot steel tank on Commercial Street burst open. [4s]
> Two point three million gallons. A wave some said was fifteen feet high, moving around thirty-five miles an hour. [6s]
> The tank had leaked for years. The company's fix? They painted it brown. [4s]
> Then physics made it worse. [1.5s]
> In the January cold, the molasses thickened as it spread, trapping people and slowing rescuers. [5s]
> A court later found the company responsible. [3s]
