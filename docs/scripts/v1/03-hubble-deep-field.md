# 3. Hubble stared at nothing

**Hook (0-2s):** black paper; torn hole opens on empty dark sky; clippings "NOTHING"

## VO
> In 1995, the director of the Hubble Space Telescope pointed it at nothing. [4s]
> A patch of sky so empty, it was about the width of a dime seen from seventy-five feet away. [5s]
> He was advised it would be a waste. He used his own discretionary time anyway. [4s]
> For ten days, Hubble stared. Three hundred and forty-two exposures. [4s]
> And the nothing came back full. [2s]
> About three thousand galaxies. [2s]
> Almost every dot in this picture is a galaxy, each one full of stars. [4s]
> Nothing turned out to be everything. [2.5s]
