# 2. The penny is dead

**Hook (0-2s):** a penny cutout, marker writes "3.69¢" next to it; clippings "COSTS 3.69¢"

## VO
> This penny cost almost four cents to make. [3s]
> In 2024, the US Mint lost eighty-five million dollars making pennies. [4s]
> It had lost money on the penny for nineteen years straight. [3s]
> Because a penny isn't really copper. It's ninety-seven and a half percent zinc, with a thin copper coat. And zinc got expensive. [7s]
> So on November twelfth, 2025, in Philadelphia, America struck its last penny. [5s]
> After two hundred and thirty-two years. [2.5s]
> Here's the twist. The nickel costs almost fourteen cents to make. [4s]
