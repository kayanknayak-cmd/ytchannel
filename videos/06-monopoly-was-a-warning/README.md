# 06. Monopoly was a warning

Niche: business

## Script
> Monopoly was invented as a warning. Against, basically, everything you do in Monopoly. In 1904, a woman named Lizzie Magie patented The Landlord's Game, to show how owning all the land makes a few people rich and everyone else broke. Thirty years later, a guy named Charles Darrow sold his version to Parker Brothers and said he came up with it. They bought her patent too. For five hundred bucks. Which is why almost nobody knows ||

`||` marks the loop point: the last line runs straight into the first.
