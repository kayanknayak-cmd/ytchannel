# 20. Tulip mania didn't happen

Niche: econ

## Script
> You've probably heard tulips once crashed the Dutch economy. It pretty much didn't happen. Okay, there was a tulip bubble in the 1630s. But a historian went through the actual records, and found fewer than four hundred people she could confirm were trading. Mostly rich merchants who could afford to lose.
