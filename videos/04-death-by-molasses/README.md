# 04. Death by molasses

Niche: science

## Script
> Twenty-one people in Boston were killed by molasses. January 1919. A giant tank of it burst. Two point three million gallons, in a wave people said was fifteen feet tall. And that tank had been leaking for years. The company's fix? Paint it brown, so you couldn't see the leaks. Then the cold kicked in.
