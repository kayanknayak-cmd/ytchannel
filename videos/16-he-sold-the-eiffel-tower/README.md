# 16. He sold the Eiffel Tower

Niche: business

## Script
> A con man sold the Eiffel Tower. Twice. Well, once and a half. 1925, Victor Lustig pretends to be a French government official and tells scrap dealers the tower costs too much to keep up, so it's being sold for scrap. Top secret. One dealer bites, and even slips him a bribe. Then he's too embarrassed to call the police. So Lustig comes back and tries it again. That one didn't work. That's why people say ||

`||` marks the loop point: the last line runs straight into the first.
