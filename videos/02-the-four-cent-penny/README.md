# 02. The four-cent penny

Niche: econ

## Script
> In 2024, it cost the US almost four cents to make one penny. Not a typo. The Mint lost eighty-five million dollars on pennies that year alone. A penny's basically zinc with a thin copper coat, and zinc got expensive. So in November 2025, they struck the last one. After two hundred and thirty-two years. Oh, and the nickel? Almost fourteen cents. And the penny kept losing money, because ||

`||` marks the loop point: the last line runs straight into the first.
