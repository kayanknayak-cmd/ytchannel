# 03. A picture of nothing

Niche: science

## Script
> This is a picture of nothing. In 1995, they pointed Hubble at a patch of sky that looked completely empty. Tiny. Like a dime from seventy-five feet away. People said it'd be a waste of telescope time. They did it anyway. Ten days. Three hundred and forty-two exposures. And almost every dot you're looking at is a galaxy. About three thousand of them. In a spot that was supposed to be empty. So technically, ||

`||` marks the loop point: the last line runs straight into the first.
